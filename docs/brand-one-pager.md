# XWH AI Solo Studio · Brand One-Pager

版本 / Version: 1.0

## 一句话定位 / One-line positioning

**XWH AI 是一位独立运营者的自动化工作室：先用合成数据把范围与风险讲清楚，再为小团队交付系统连接、数据流程和有限场景的 AI 工具。**
**XWH AI is a solo automation studio: it clarifies scope and risk with synthetic data before delivering system integrations, data workflows, and narrowly scoped AI tools for small teams.**

## 目标客户 / Intended customers

- 需要减少重复录入、手工汇总或跨系统搬运的小团队。
  Small teams that want to reduce repetitive entry, manual consolidation, or cross-system copying.
- 有明确业务流程，但缺少专职开发或运维人员的小微企业与内部项目组。
  Small businesses and internal teams with a defined workflow but no dedicated developer or operator.
- 能够提供业务负责人、必要系统权限和测试时间的客户。
  Customers that can provide a business owner, necessary system permissions, and time for testing.
- 暂不包括需要高风险自主决策、受监管身份核验或全天候值守的场景。
  High-risk autonomous decision-making, regulated identity verification, and around-the-clock operations are not standard offerings.

## 核心价值 / Core value

1. **范围先于承诺 / Scope before promises**
   先定义输入、输出、异常与验收，再讨论实现。
   Inputs, outputs, exceptions, and acceptance criteria are defined before implementation.

2. **可解释、可交接 / Explainable and transferable**
   交付约定代码、配置说明、测试记录和运行手册。
   Agreed code, configuration notes, test records, and an operating guide are handed over.

3. **数据默认最小化 / Data-minimizing by default**
   演示与验证优先使用合成数据；真实数据须经批准并限制用途。
   Synthetic data is preferred for demonstrations and validation; real data requires approval and restricted use.

4. **人工兜底 / Human fallback**
   AI 与自动化用于起草、分类和提示；关键动作保留人工确认。
   AI and automation draft, classify, or suggest; important actions retain human review.

## 明确边界 / Explicit boundaries

- 不承诺固定收益、业务增长或完全无误的结果。
  No fixed return, business growth, or error-free result is promised.
- 不承诺削减员工或改变组织编制；交付的是有边界的流程工具。
  No staffing reduction or organizational change is promised; deliverables are bounded workflow tools.
- 不处理凭据收集、绕过访问控制、欺骗性交互或违法数据用途。
  No credential harvesting, access-control bypass, deceptive interaction, or unlawful data use is handled.
- 人脸识别不是标准服务，须先进行单独的法律、同意与风险评估。
  Facial recognition is not a standard service and requires a separate legal, consent, and risk review.
- 当前为个人接单，**无法开具发票；客户必须开票时不接单**。
  Work is currently undertaken by an individual who **cannot issue invoices; work requiring an invoice is declined**.
