# XWH AI 常见问题
# XWH AI FAQ

版本 / Version: 1.0

以下回答以公开、诚实的边界为主，不构成真实客户案例、效果数据、资质证明或法律意见。
The answers below emphasize public, honest boundaries and do not represent real customer cases, outcome metrics, certifications, or legal advice.

## 1. 你能保证自动化带来收益吗？ / Can you guarantee a return from automation?

**答 / Answer:** 不能。收益取决于流程量、数据质量、系统配合和实际使用情况。
**Answer:** No. Outcomes depend on workflow volume, data quality, system cooperation, and actual adoption.

可以先记录人工步骤、处理批次、异常数等基线，上线后比较；仍可能没有明显改善。
We can first record a baseline of manual steps, batch volume, and exceptions, then compare it after launch; improvement may still be limited.

**承诺边界 / Commitment boundary:** 承诺约定范围的实现与验收，不承诺业务增长或固定回报。
**Commitment boundary:** Implementation and acceptance of agreed scope are committed; business growth or fixed returns are not.

## 2. 能减少多少人工工作？ / How much manual work can be reduced?

**答 / Answer:** 不承诺削减员工或改变组织编制。交付目标是减少重复步骤，并把异常交给人工。
**Answer:** Staffing reductions or organizational changes are not promised. The delivery goal is to reduce repetitive steps and route exceptions to people.

关键判断、客户投诉、付款和身份相关动作需要客户定义人工复核。
Critical decisions, complaints, payments, and identity-related actions need customer-defined human review.

## 3. AI 客服会不会答错？ / Can an AI support agent give wrong answers?

**答 / Answer:** 会。模型可能误解问题、引用过期内容或生成不准确文本。
**Answer:** Yes. A model may misunderstand a question, use outdated content, or generate inaccurate text.

因此只基于批准知识库生成草稿，设置拒答、转人工和知识更新机制，并用合成问题测试。
It therefore drafts from an approved knowledge base, with refusal, escalation, and knowledge-update mechanisms tested using synthetic questions.

**承诺边界 / Commitment boundary:** 不承诺无限知识覆盖或完全无误；高风险问题不自动终局处理。
**Commitment boundary:** Unlimited knowledge coverage and error-free output are not promised; high-risk questions do not receive autonomous final handling.

## 4. 你们有资质或客户案例吗？ / Do you have certifications or customer cases?

**答 / Answer:** 本公开资料不宣称任何资质、认证、真实客户或案例结果。
**Answer:** This public material claims no certification, credential, real customer, or case result.

能力状态以 [能力矩阵](capability-matrix.md) 为准；只有可复现的合成演示和测试证据才能支持 Verified demo 标记。
Capability status follows the [capability matrix](capability-matrix.md); only a reproducible synthetic demo and test evidence can support a Verified demo label.

## 5. 你们能用我们的数据做模型吗？ / Can you train a model on our data?

**答 / Answer:** 不把客户数据用于通用模型训练、微调、蒸馏或公开演示。
**Answer:** Customer data is not used to train, fine-tune, distill, or publicly demonstrate a general model.

客户特定模型项目必须另案确认授权、环境、许可、删除与第三方条款，不能默认开展。
Customer-specific model work requires separate authorization, environment, license, deletion, and third-party review; it is not a default service.

## 6. 如何验证能力又不暴露数据？ / How can you validate capability without exposing data?

**答 / Answer:** 使用虚构姓名、组织、编号和业务场景构建合成样本，检查正常与异常流程。
**Answer:** Use synthetic samples with fictional names, organizations, identifiers, and business scenarios, then test normal and abnormal flows.

真实数据只有在合法授权、最小必要和书面用途确认后才可能接收。
Real data is accepted only after lawful authorization, minimum-necessity review, and written purpose confirmation.

## 7. 数据归谁？项目结束后会删除吗？ / Who owns the data, and is it deleted?

**答 / Answer:** 客户数据归客户或合法权利人；XWH AI 不主张其所有权。
**Answer:** Customer data belongs to the customer or lawful rights holder; XWH AI claims no ownership.

默认在项目结束或客户提前要求后 30 个自然日内删除工作副本，备份按批准的最长 90 天轮转期清除；客户系统原始数据由客户管理。
By default, working copies are deleted within 30 calendar days after project end or earlier request; backups expire after an approved rotation period of no more than 90 days; source data in customer systems is customer-managed.

详细条件见 [安全与数据规范](security-and-data.md)。
See the [security and data policy](security-and-data.md) for full conditions.

## 8. 你们会公开我们的名称、截图或结果吗？ / Will you publish our name, screenshots, or results?

**答 / Answer:** 不会未经明确书面授权公开。默认只使用合成或通用材料。
**Answer:** Not without explicit written authorization. Synthetic or generic materials are the default.

即使获授权，也会另案审查范围、内容与脱敏，不以口头同意直接发布。
Even with authorization, scope, content, and sanitization are reviewed separately; verbal consent alone does not trigger publication.

## 9. 能做人脸识别吗？ / Do you offer facial recognition?

**答 / Answer:** 人脸识别不是标准服务，当前仅列为 Experimental，通常不接。
**Answer:** Facial recognition is not a standard service; it is currently Experimental and usually declined.

任何涉及人脸数据的讨论都要先确认合法性、授权、必要性、保存期限和更安全替代方案。
Any facial-data discussion first requires legality, authorization, necessity, retention, and safer alternatives to be confirmed.

不提供监控、追踪、身份终局判断或未经同意的数据采集。
Surveillance, tracking, final identity decisions, and collection without consent are not offered.

## 10. 为什么先报价区间，不直接给固定价？ / Why give a price range instead of a fixed price?

**答 / Answer:** 系统接口、权限、数据质量、异常和验收范围会显著改变工作量。
**Answer:** Interfaces, permissions, data quality, exceptions, and acceptance scope can significantly change effort.

体检后可以把范围收敛为固定价或更窄的区间；范围变化再走书面变更。
After an audit, scope may be converted to a fixed price or narrower range; changes then follow written change control.

参考价见 [服务目录](service-catalog.md)。
See the [service catalog](service-catalog.md) for reference prices.

## 11. 能开发票吗？ / Can you issue an invoice?

**答 / Answer:** 当前个人接单，**无法开具发票；客户必须开票时不接单**。
**Answer:** Work is currently individual and **invoicing is not available; work requiring an invoice is declined**.

这个限制在筛选阶段确认，不接受以个人收据、另找第三方或隐瞒开票要求代替。
This limitation is confirmed during qualification; personal receipts, another party, or concealed invoicing needs are not substitutes.

## 12. 你们能全天候响应吗？ / Do you provide 24/7 response?

**答 / Answer:** 不能把一人公司描述为全天候团队。
**Answer:** A solo operator cannot be presented as a 24/7 team.

月度维护可以约定工作日时段和确认响应目标，但不是全天候 SLA；紧急故障应有客户侧停机、人工接管和回滚方案。
Monthly maintenance may define weekday hours and an acknowledgment target, but not a 24/7 SLA; the customer needs shutdown, human-takeover, and rollback plans for urgent failures.

## 13. 第三方 API 或网页界面变化怎么办？ / What if an API or web interface changes?

**答 / Answer:** 第三方变化不在单方控制内。任务可能失败、需要调整，甚至因条款变化必须停止。
**Answer:** Third-party changes are not unilaterally controllable. A task may fail, need adjustment, or have to stop due to changed terms.

交付会记录依赖、错误处理和人工接管；变化后先评估影响，再决定修正、变更或替代方案。
Delivery records dependencies, error handling, and human takeover; changes are assessed before repair, change order, or replacement.

RPA 通常比 API 更依赖界面稳定性，但不承诺任何界面永远不变。
RPA is generally more UI-dependent than APIs, but no interface is promised to remain unchanged.

## 14. 谁负责代码和知识产权？ / Who owns the code and intellectual property?

**答 / Answer:** 数据归属与代码/文档知识产权是两个问题，具体归属写入报价或协议。
**Answer:** Data ownership and code/document intellectual property are separate issues; ownership is written into the quote or agreement.

第三方软件、模型、字体、图片和数据仍受原许可约束，不因项目交付自动转让。
Third-party software, models, fonts, images, and data remain subject to their original licenses and are not automatically transferred.

## 15. 出现数据或密钥事故怎么处理？ / What happens in a data or key incident?

**答 / Answer:** 先暂停相关流程、撤销或轮换受影响访问、保留最小证据，再向约定联系人通报已知事实。
**Answer:** The affected workflow is paused, affected access is revoked or rotated, minimum evidence is retained, and known facts are reported to the designated contact.

确认影响后的内部通知目标为 24 小时，初步复盘目标为 5 个工作日；具体法定义务和合同责任仍需按项目确认。
The internal notification target is 24 hours after impact is confirmed, with a preliminary review target of five business days; legal and contractual duties still depend on the project.

完整步骤见 [安全与数据规范](security-and-data.md#事故处理--incident-handling)。
See the full [incident process](security-and-data.md#事故处理--incident-handling).

## 16. 可以先做一个很短的验证吗？ / Can we start with a very small validation?

**答 / Answer:** 可以讨论 Scope before delivery 或 Experimental 验证，但应先限定目标、数据、预算和停止线。
**Answer:** A Scope before delivery or Experimental validation can be discussed after defining the goal, data, budget, and stop line.

短验证不等于免费开发，也不会自动承诺后续上线。
A short validation is not free development and does not automatically commit to later launch.

## 17. 网站部署后，国内三网都能访问吗？ / Will the site be reachable on all three mainland Chinese networks?

**答 / Answer:** 不预设结论。域名、节点、网络和运营商情况会变化，必须用实际测试记录。
**Answer:** No conclusion is assumed. Domains, nodes, networks, and carrier conditions change; actual test records are required.

上线前必须分别通过电信、联通、移动的 DNS、HTTPS、首页、静态资源和关键交互检查；任一失败就不正式发布。
Before launch, DNS, HTTPS, homepage, static assets, and key interactions must pass separate checks on China Telecom, China Unicom, and China Mobile; any failure blocks official launch.

流程见 [部署文档](deployment.md#五三网可达性硬闸--5-three-network-reachability-hard-gate)。
See the [deployment guide](deployment.md#五三网可达性硬闸--5-three-network-reachability-hard-gate).
