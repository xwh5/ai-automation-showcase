Brief 01 / Operating context

A component is only as reliable as the record behind it.

Small-batch manufacturing often scopes information across email, spreadsheets, and inspection reports. Buyers do not need more files. They need fast answers: what material is this, which drawing revision governed it, who released it, and how were exceptions handled?

This synthetic case organizes those questions into one evidence chain. The website does more than display products: it explains workflows, deliverables, and control points so a visitor can assess fit before making contact.

From drawing to release, every step has a home.

Lot journey

    Confirm drawing & variant

    Capture revision, material, and critical tolerances before work starts.

  2. 2

    Bind material evidence

    Link the material certificate and incoming check to the same lot.

  3. 3

    Complete first-article inspection

    Use a fixed dimensional sheet instead of a vague “passed” note.

  4. 4

    Resolve exceptions & release

    Keep exceptions visible until evidence is complete and a named role reviews them.
